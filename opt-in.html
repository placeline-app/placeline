Opt-in steps

  1. A senior placement professional asks Michail Stankosky to set up PlaceLine for her work.

  2. She is shown the SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.

  3. She opts in by texting this consent message from her own phone to the PlaceLine number, (844) 730-4628:

    "Yes, I agree to receive automated messages from PlaceLine at this number, including replies to my notes, follow up reminders and a daily task list. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."

  4. PlaceLine replies with a confirmation:

    "PlaceLine: You're signed up for replies and reminders about your work. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."

  5. No messages are sent to anyone who has not completed these steps. Phone numbers are never bought, imported from lists, or added without consent. Replying STOP at any time ends all messages.

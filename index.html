About

PlaceLine helps a senior placement professional keep track of the families and communities she works with. She texts notes or photos of her notes to her PlaceLine number. PlaceLine organizes them in her customer records and texts her back with a short summary to approve, plus reminders when a follow-up is due.

PlaceLine is a private service. It does not send marketing messages, and it does not text families, seniors or communities. It only messages the enrolled user who signed up for it.

SMS Terms

Who receives messages

Only enrolled users of PlaceLine. Today that is one person: a senior placement professional who asked for the service.

How users opt in

Users sign up directly with Michail Stankosky and give written consent to receive automated text messages from the PlaceLine number before any messages are sent. Phone numbers are never purchased, collected from lists, or added without consent.

What messages are sent

  • Replies to the user's own texts, such as a summary of notes to approve
  • Follow-up reminders the user asked for
  • A morning list of the day's follow-ups

Example: "I'll save this: Conner family moved to Touring, tour Friday 3:30 PM, reminder to call Monday. OK?"

Example: "Good morning! Your calls today: 1) Reuben family 2) Falls River Village. Reply STOP to end messages."

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with the user's activity, typically a few messages per day. Message and data rates may apply.

How to stop or get help

Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the details below.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026.

Information we collect

The enrolled user's name and mobile phone number, the messages and photos she sends to the PlaceLine number, and the notes and reminders created from them.

How we use it

Only to provide the service: understanding the user's messages, updating her own customer records, and sending her replies and reminders.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run PlaceLine: a text-messaging provider, an AI provider that reads and summarizes messages, and the user's own customer-records (CRM) system. They process information only to provide the service.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We do not sell personal information.

Security

Access is limited to the enrolled user's phone number and to the operator. Account keys are stored privately and never published.

Your choices

Reply STOP to stop messages. To have your information deleted, contact us below.
